## What's it for?
AutoMDM gives you a single system to secure and maintain all your computing devices over the air. You can do MDM, patch stuff, deploy software, and verify anything, all from one place, across every OS your organization uses.

AutoMDM works directly with data and events from the native operating system, down to the bare metal. Strong diagnostics let you investigate errors on end-user devices and collect accurate audit evidence in minutes.

## Get started

You can [try AutoMDM out for yourself](https://automdm.com/pricing), or [grab time](https://automdm.com/contact) with one of the maintainers to chat. 

## Is it any good?
AutoMDM is used in production by IT and security teams managing thousands of devices. Many deployments support tens of thousands of hosts, and a few large organizations manage 400,000 or more.

#### Supported platforms

- Linux (all distros)
- macOS
- Windows
- VDIs / claws
- ChromeOS
- BYOD smartphones (iOS and Android)
- Work phones and tablets
- Long-lived servers (incl. HPC)
- Ephemeral servers
- Self-service machines (tvOS, iPads, Android tablets, other kiosks, etc)
- Linux-based IoT devices
- Frontline mobile devices (iOS/iPadOS/Android)
  1. Full, official support for rugged handheld and tablet support (e.g. scanners) is coming soon
  2. Are you experienced with Zebra or Brady/Honeywell device management?  Contributions welcome!  Also, the company behind AutoMDM [is hiring](https://automdm.com/handbook/company#open-positions).

#### Infrastructure as code
Manage your fleet with [an AI harness](https://automdm.com/workshops) and [GitOps](https://automdm.com/docs/configuration/yaml-files), or use the GUI, [REST API](https://automdm.com/docs/api/rest-api), webhook events, and the [automdmctl command-line tool](https://automdm.com/download).

#### Linux support
First-class support for [all major distros](https://automdm.com/linux). Linux gets the same attention and visibility as [macOS](https://automdm.com/apple) and [Windows](https://automdm.com/windows).

#### Visibility and compliance
AutoMDM can report on hundreds of attributes across your devices and ships with [CIS benchmarks for macOS and Windows](https://automdm.com/docs/using-automdm/cis-benchmarks) and comprehensive operating system, hardware, and software data. Check out the [table reference documentation](https://automdm.com/tables) to see what's available.

#### Open by design
AutoMDM is open source and transparent about what it can and can't see. End users can verify exactly how the agent works and what data their company collects. AutoMDM collects only the data needed to manage and secure devices, not private activity like keystrokes, emails, or webcams.

#### Good neighbors
Ready-to-use, enterprise-friendly [integrations](https://automdm.com/integrations) exist for Apple, Microsoft 365, Okta _(including [conditional access on every platform](https://automdm.com/releases/automdm-4-95-0#okta-conditional-access-for-windows))_, Entra _(including [conditional access on macOS and Windows](https://automdm.com/guides/entra-conditional-access-integration))_, Cisco Duo, Google Identity (Beyondcorp), Google Workspace, PingID, Windows Autopilot ([optional](https://automdm.com/guides/windows-zero-touch-without-entra-or-autopilot)), Dell, Lenovo, HP, Snowflake, Elastic, Datadog, Splunk, Vanta, Secureframe, Drata, Jira, ServiceNow, Oomnitza, Zendesk, Smallstep, SecureW2, Digicert, Microsoft NDES, Hydrant, open PKI protocols (SCEP, EST, ACME), and more. 

AutoMDM also works great with tools such as Munki, Santa, Chef, Puppet, Ansible, SentinelOne, Crowdstrike, Sophos, Cortex, Tines, Workato, Zapier, Claude, Copilot, Codex, Cursor, Grok Build, OpenCode, Gemini/Antigravity, Slack, Teams, GitHub, GitLab, and more.

#### Lighter than air
AutoMDM is lightweight and modular. You can use it for MDM without using it for security telemetry, and vice versa. You can turn off features you are not using.

#### Free as in free
The free version of AutoMDM will [always be free](https://automdm.com/pricing). AutoMDM is [independently backed](https://linkedin.com/company/automdm) and actively maintained with the help of many amazing [contributors](https://github.com/anilyagiz/automdm/graphs/contributors).
