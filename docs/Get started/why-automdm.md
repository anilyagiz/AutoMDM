# Why AutoMDM

AutoMDM is open-source AI-native device management, patching, and governance for every OS: macOS, Windows, Linux, Chromebooks, iOS, and Android (BYOD or corporate-owned).

Get work done natively with your team's AI tools, like Claude, Copilot, or any AI harness.

![Claude checks AutoMDM and opens a pull request with the changes needed for CIS Level 2 on Macs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/automdm/automdm/main/docs/images/claude-proposes-automdm-change-612x356@2x.gif)

## What's it for?

AutoMDM gives you a single system to secure and maintain all your computing devices over the air. You can do MDM, patch stuff, deploy software, and verify anything–all from one place, across every OS and device your organization uses.

AutoMDM is open source, and free features will always be free.

## Is it any good?

AutoMDM is used in production by IT and security teams managing thousands of devices. Many deployments support tens of thousands, and a few large organizations manage 400,000 or more.

Bring the AI tool you already trust, like Copilot, Claude, or ChatGPT. Tell it what you need in your own words, like "Our security team needs to know which Windows computers don't have antivirus protection running." It works out the change for you, whether that's a policy, report, or configuration profile. A person on your team approves every change before it reaches any device. To get started, see [Connect your AI tool to AutoMDM](https://automdm.com/guides/connect-your-ai-tool-to-automdm).

- **Get what you need:** AutoMDM works directly with data and events from the native operating system, down to the bare metal. Strong diagnostics let you investigate errors on end-user devices and collect accurate audit evidence in minutes. It’s also modular. (You can turn off features you are not using.)
- **Out of the box:** Ready-to-use integrations exist for [most common tools](https://automdm.com/integrations). You can become a power user in the GUI or govern your fleet with [AI](https://automdm.com/guides/connect-your-ai-tool-to-automdm). You can also build custom workflows with the REST API, webhook events, MCP server, and the automdmctl command-line tool. 
- **Good neighbors:** AutoMDM is independent and open by design. We helped [create osquery](https://automdm.com/handbook/company#history) and remain committed to improving it.
- **Free as in free:** The free version of AutoMDM will [always be free](https://automdm.com/pricing). AutoMDM is independently backed and actively maintained with the help of many contributors.

## Scope transparency

AutoMDM is transparent about what it can and can't see. You can verify exactly how the agent works. End users can see what the agent is capable of and what kinds of data their company chooses to collect.

AutoMDM is designed to collect only the data needed to manage and secure devices: things like user accounts, device health, installed software, and security settings. It isn't built to log private activity such as keystrokes, emails, or webcams.

This way, all users share the same understanding of how devices are monitored and secured.

[Install AutoMDM](https://automdm.com/docs/deploy/deploy-automdm) on your own server, or [let us host it](https://automdm.com/pricing) for you. Enroll your devices, and you're set.
